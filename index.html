      Move: WASD   Jump: Space   Dock: hold Shift   Tether / untether: Q next to a player   Grab a tethered friend: hold E   Chat: Enter   Camera: drag the mouse or arrow keys.
      Tether up with as many friends as you like (Q toggles a rope to the nearest player). Everyone roped together must reach the finish, and the more of you there are, the shorter the ropes get. A spike or the void sends the whole group back to the start. Big gap? Dock, have your partner run away to stretch the rope, then let go - they fly back at you. Hold E to grab them as they pass, jump together, and the rider can hit Space mid-air for a second hop.
